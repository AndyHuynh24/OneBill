Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

OneBill helps you scan receipts, split bills with friends, and keep track of subscriptions and bills. This page explains what OneBill stores, why, and the choices you have.

What OneBill stores

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • What you add: what OneBill reads from your receipts and bills (store, items, amounts, dates), the names you type when splitting a bill, and subscriptions or bills you add.
  • Photos and PDFs aren't kept: each one is deleted right after OneBill has read it (anything left over is removed automatically within a day). Only what was read is saved.
  • Emails you choose to send: if you connect Gmail, a script that you install in your own Google account sends only the emails you label "OneBill". OneBill never reads the rest of your inbox.

How it's used

  • Only to provide OneBill's features: reading your receipts and bills, splitting them, and showing what's due.
  • To read a receipt, OneBill uses Amazon Textract and an AI model on Amazon Bedrock. Names, addresses, and card numbers are masked before the AI sees anything. AWS doesn't use this data to train models.
  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data, no tracking across other sites.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1), encrypted at rest and in transit. The website's files are served by Google Firebase Hosting, which doesn't receive your bills or account data.

Google user data

With "Continue with Google", OneBill receives only your email address, name, and profile picture to create your account. OneBill's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your browser

OneBill keeps your sign-in and a few preferences (such as recent friends' names) in your browser's storage. It doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Delete any bill in the app at any time (open it, then "Delete bill").
  • To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us. It's done within 30 days.
  • If you connected Gmail, you can stop it at any time by removing the script from your Google account or revoking its key in OneBill.

Children

OneBill isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes will be shown in the app.
